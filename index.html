Anima r1 concept 

The Anima R1 RWD is a rear-wheel-drive sports sedan powered by Anima Apex-4 (OEM: Mercedes-AMG M139) engine core. Engineered with a longitudinal powertrain layout, traditional 6-speed H-pattern manual transmission, and lightweight chassis dynamics, the R1 delivers supercar-grade performance in a focused, high-rigidity package.
