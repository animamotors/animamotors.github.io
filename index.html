ENGINEERING BRIEFS & CHASSIS ARCHITECTURE

The Anima R1 RWD is a rear-wheel-drive sports sedan powered by Anima Apex-4 (OEM: Mercedes-AMG M139) engine core. Engineered with a longitudinal powertrain layout, traditional 6-speed H-pattern manual transmission, and lightweight chassis dynamics, the R1 delivers supercar-grade performance in a focused, high-rigidity package.

Designed from first principles to prioritize mechanical steering feel, progressive breakaway characteristics at the limit, and an unmistakable early-2000s low-slung beltline silhouette.

ORIGIN & PHILOSOPHY

THE ANALOGUE SOUL OF MOTORSPORT

The early 2000s marked the pinnacle of driver-centric sports cars — an era of unfiltered mechanical feedback, balanced hydraulic steering racks, and pure communion between driver, transmission, and tarmac.

Over the last two decades, performance motoring has become increasingly digitized, isolated behind synthetic drive-by-wire layers, electronic intervention systems, and excessive battery mass.

ANIMA MOTORS™ was founded on a singular conviction: drivers crave tactile mechanical emotion. By uniting the visceral engagement of early-2000s Japanese and European GT homologation specials with modern carbon composite engineering, the Anima R1 re-establishes what a true driver's car should feel like.

01 // TACTILE
6-Speed Manual
Mechanical linkage, physical H-pattern gate, and weighted knob with zero artificial rev-hang.
02 // BALANCE
50:50 Dynamic Mass
Longitudinal front-mid engine layout for predictable, knife-edge yaw response at the limit.
03 // GRIP
Mechanical LSD
Clutch-type limited slip differential providing honest, progressive power transfer without electronic brake pulsing.
04 // AERO
Venturi Channels
True ground-effect underfloor aerodynamics generating high-speed downforce with minimal drag penalty.
