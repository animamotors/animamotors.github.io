Anima R1

A rear-wheel-drive sports sedan with a manual gearbox and no driver-assist filters.

Engineering

The Anima R1 is a rear-wheel-drive sports sedan powered by the Apex-4 engine core (OEM: Mercedes-AMG M139). Built with a longitudinal powertrain layout, a 6-speed manual transmission, and lightweight chassis dynamics, the R1 delivers supercar performance in a high-rigidity package.

Designed to prioritize mechanical steering feel, predictable breakaway characteristics at the limit, and an early-2000s low-slung beltline silhouette.
